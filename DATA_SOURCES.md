# Data sources for the female model

Checked on 2026-11-20 in the order the brief requested. "Used" means the data is in `public/models/female/`.

## a) BodyParts3D / FMA archive (DBCLS) — **used for shared structures only**

- URL: https://dbarchive.biosciencedbc.jp/en/bodyparts3d/download.html (`isa_*` and `partof_*` tables, `isa_BP3D_4.0_obj_99.zip`)
- License: **CC BY 4.0** (https://dbarchive.biosciencedbc.jp/en/bodyparts3d/lic.html, updated 2025-02-27). The OBJ comments still cite the older CC BY-SA 2.1 JP text; the current database license supersedes it.
- Female content: **none.** I searched `isa_element_parts.txt` for uterus, ovary, vagina, mammary, breast, clitoris, labium, vulva, female and fallopian, with no matches. Male-only elements are present: testis FJ3138/3142, prostate FJ3139, penis FJ3132–3134, seminal vesicles, epididymides and deferent ducts. The single model is an adult male (TARO MRI).
- Use: every sex-neutral structure (2,206 elements), scaled. The male pelvis is morphed (DECISIONS.md D4).

## b) Z-Anatomy — **not used**

- URL: https://github.com/Z-Anatomy/Models-of-human-anatomy, https://www.z-anatomy.com/
- License: the repository is **CC BY-SA 4.0** overall. Its README also credits third-party additions under other licenses, including "Anatomy of the Inner Ear" (CC BY-NC-SA 4.0) and "Kidney" (CC BY-NC 4.0). The other credit listed is "Cranial Nerves and Foramina" (CC BY 4.0).
- Female content: Z-Anatomy is built on the BodyParts3D male. Its listed additions (cranial nerves, inner ear, kidney) include no female reproductive structures.
- Reason not used: it adds no female structures, and the mixed share-alike and non-commercial terms would complicate redistribution. No Z-Anatomy data is in this repository, so no share-alike obligations apply.

## d) Generated structures (last resort) — **used for 16 structures**

No openly licensed scan-derived source was found for these, so each is generated by a reproducible Blender 4.2 script and labelled *approximate / schematic, not scan-derived* in metadata and in the UI:

| Structure | Script | Reference dimensions used |
|---|---|---|
| Labia majora (L/R) | `create_vulva.py` | Length 70–120 mm (85 used) — Lloyd J, Crouch NS, Minto CL, Liao LM, Creighton SM. *Female genital appearance: "normality" unfolds.* BJOG 2005;112:643–646 |
| Labia minora (L/R) | `create_vulva.py` | Length 20–100 mm (45 used), width 7–50 mm (~11 used) — Lloyd et al. 2005 |
| Vestibule of vagina (with urethral and vaginal openings) | `create_vulva.py` | Clitoris–urethra distance 16–45 mm (~19 used) — Lloyd et al. 2005 |
| Glans, body, left/right crus of clitoris | `create_clitoris.py` | Glans width 3–10 mm (7 used) — Lloyd et al. 2005. Body bends toward the symphysis; crura follow the ischiopubic rami — O'Connell HE, Hutson JM, Anderson CR, Plenter RJ. *Anatomical relationship between urethra and clitoris.* J Urol 1998;159:1892–1897; O'Connell HE, Sanjeevan KV, Hutson JM. *Anatomy of the clitoris.* J Urol 2005;174:1189–1195 |
| Vestibular bulbs (L/R) | `create_clitoris.py` | Lateral to the vestibule, deep to the labia — O'Connell et al. 2005; Gray's Anatomy |
| Female urethra | `create_female_urethra.py` | About 4 cm long, 6 mm diameter, behind the symphysis in the anterior vaginal wall — Standring S (ed.), *Gray's Anatomy*, 42nd ed., Elsevier 2020 |
| Ischiocavernosus, bulbospongiosus (L/R, female) | `create_perineal_muscles.py` | Cover the crura and the vestibular bulbs respectively — Gray's Anatomy, 42nd ed. |

Positions are not free-floating. They are computed from real neighbouring geometry (`build_female.py: vulva_landmarks`):
- the vaginal orifice, where the HRA vagina's axis meets the adapted skin
- the inferior pubic symphysis and ischiopubic rami of the morphed pelvis
- the HRA bladder neck
- the external anal sphincter

Each structure is collision-checked against bone and the HRA organs with python-fcl, pushed clear, and re-checked (VERIFICATION.md).

## Ontology identifiers

FMA IDs come from the EBI Ontology Lookup Service (https://www.ebi.ac.uk/ols4): UBERON `database_cross_reference` entries and exact FMA label matches. They are cached in `scripts/female/ontology_xrefs.json` (243 of 256 HRA UBERON terms have an FMA cross-reference). Where no FMA term exists, the HRA UBERON ID or a `GEN:` ID is kept.

## Other references used for decisions

- Stature: Fryar CD, Carroll MD, Gu Q, Afful J, Ogden CL. *Anthropometric reference data for children and adults: United States, 2015–2018.* Natl Health Stat Report 2021;(160). Used for comparison only (D3).
- Pelvic sexual dimorphism (direction of the morph targets): Gray's Anatomy 42nd ed.; and standard obstetric pelvimetry: interspinous ~24 cm, intertuberous ~11 cm in females against ~8 cm in males, subpubic angle ~80–90° against ~50–60°.
